Current fees and rules

Good to know before you start

The fees and rules that matter most when you start, in plain words.

Top-up fee: 3.9%, taken from what you load

The fee now comes out of the amount you load, so load $100 and $96.10 reaches your card. The minimum top-up is $10.

Special Edition: $370

Choose it when you buy your card and it costs $370. Upgrading a regular card to the Special Edition later costs $470, so decide at the start.

Your first star

Everyone starts as a Member. Your first star comes when you hold a Premium card and directly refer two new participants who each buy a Premium card.

Keep your account safe

Bellver never emails links to its login page. Use a strong password and switch on two-factor login or a passkey from day one.

Physical cards: two must-haves

Give a complete, accurate shipping address and a valid mobile number from the country where the card will be delivered. The courier uses both, and without them the card cannot be delivered.

Keep a little BNB for fees

Network fees on BNB Chain are paid in BNB. Keep about $10 worth in your wallet so every step goes through.

Built for people who go live

If you host TikTok LIVEs, your audience already knows how you talk about products and services. That trust took you years to build, and it is the hardest part of sharing anything.

One of the best fits

TikTok LIVE hosts and creators

  • Show it, do not just say it. Pay for something live, add the card to your phone, show the dashboard (never your private key).
  • Your viewers already trust you. People who watch you regularly believe what you tell them, so honesty matters most.
  • Something real to offer. A card with high limits and a wallet they control, plus a clear commission table and a link that tracks.
  • Stay within the rules. Disclose that you earn a commission and check TikTok's current rules for your country first.
