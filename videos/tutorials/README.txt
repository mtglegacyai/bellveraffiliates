STEPS, WITH THE EXACT ON-SCREEN LABELS
1. Log in at app.bellvercards.com. The Dashboard opens by default.
2. Dashboard, bottom row of four boxes. Explain each:
   - "Current balance on all my cards": what is on your card(s).
   - "Lifetime direct commissions": total direct commissions so far.
   - "Lifetime reward": total monthly rewards so far.
   - "Earnings available" ("Total of your earnings available for
     withdrawal"): the amount you can withdraw now. Point to it.
3. Left menu: tap "Withdraw".
4. The "Withdraw" page: "Your earnings, wallet and payout claims. Every
   figure comes from your own commission and reward ledgers."
   Show "Available to withdraw" and "Minimum payout" ($100).
   Scroll a little; explain anything important lower on the page.
5. Tap "Request payout".
6. Type the amount. The field shows the allowed range: from $100 up to
   what is available.
7. Optional "Notes" box (MTG typed: "My first withdrawal request").
8. "Pay to": your built-in Bellver wallet (Web3 wallet). It cannot be
   changed: the payout always goes to your own Bellver wallet first.
9. Enter your password, tap "Request payout". It shows "Submitting".
10. Success check: "Available to withdraw" drops to zero and a message
    says the payout request was submitted and that admin review is
    manual. The request appears in the list below with status
    "Pending".
11. After approval the amount arrives in My Wallet ("Total balance").
    The wallet address starts and ends with the same characters as the
    "Pay to" address on the Withdraw page (show both, masked in the
    middle).
12. From My Wallet you can "Send" it anywhere, for example to your own
    MetaMask:
    - choose the network: BNB Smart Chain, because fees are very low
      (the Ethereum address is the same, but fees are higher)
    - copy your MetaMask address with its copy button, paste it in
      Bellver, choose USDT, enter the amount, send
    - or "Link a new wallet" > EVM > MetaMask, switch MetaMask to BNB
      Smart Chain when asked, confirm. The linked wallet then shows.

  ends in 6611), with the line "Your Bellver wallet. Sent here once
