The card

A US dollar card account you fund with crypto and spend like any other card.

Pay worldwide

Pay in shops and online in any local currency. The card account itself is kept in US dollars.

Physical or virtual

The physical card is a Visa and the virtual card is a Mastercard. The physical card also works for cash at ATMs. The virtual card is ready as soon as you order.

Phone, ring or card

Add it to Apple Pay or Google Pay, or connect an NFC ring or wristband for tap-to-pay.

Non-custodial wallet

Your wallet. Your key.

  • Your Dynamic Wallet is yours. Created when you register, on Fireblocks. Bellver says it cannot freeze or access your funds.
  • Only you hold the private key. Export it any time.
  • Move to the card only what you plan to spend. The rest stays under your key.
  • Lock it twice: a transaction password, plus two-factor login or a passkey.
No bank to call if you lose your private key. Write it down, keep it offline, never share it.
