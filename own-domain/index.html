The written guide

Follow it click by click

Keep this page open next to GitHub and GoDaddy. Do one part at a time and tick off each check before you move on.

1

Part 1

Create your GitHub account and repository

First a free GitHub account, then a repository: a folder on GitHub that holds your website.

Create your free account

  1. Go to github.com and click Sign up.
  2. Enter your Email, create a Password and choose a Username. Write your username down: you need it in later steps.
  3. Choose your Country/Region, then click Create account.
  4. GitHub may ask you to solve a quick puzzle, then emails you a code. Enter the code, then sign in.

Create your repository

  1. Click the + at the top right, then New repository.
  2. Your username is already in the Owner box. Leave it as it is.
  3. In the Repository name box, type your username followed by .github.io. If your username is mtgtribe, type mtgtribe.github.io.
  4. Choose Public. Free GitHub accounts can only publish websites from public repositories.
  5. Turn on Add README, then click Create repository.

Why username.github.io and not just your domain name: this name puts your website at the top level, so it works and you can test it before your domain is connected.

2

Part 2

Upload your website files

Drag the contents of the folder, never the folder itself.

  1. On your computer, unzip the file from MTG. You get a folder with index.html and folders such as assets, images, docs and prices.
  2. Open that folder so you can see index.html and the other folders inside it.
  3. On GitHub, open your new repository and click Add file, then Upload files.
  4. Select everything inside the folder (Ctrl+A on Windows, Cmd+A on Mac) and drag it onto the GitHub page.
  5. Wait until every file has finished uploading, then click Commit changes.

Check: your repository now lists index.html next to folders like assets, images and prices. One folder holding everything? Delete the repository (Settings, bottom of the page) and start again, dragging the contents only.

3

Part 3

Switch on GitHub Pages and test

Your website goes live on a free github.io address first.

  1. In your repository, click Settings, then Pages in the menu on the left.
  2. Under Build and deployment, set Source to Deploy from a branch.
  3. Under Branch, choose main and / (root), then click Save.
  4. Wait 2 to 10 minutes, then open https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io.

Check: you see your Bellver website with your name, your photo and your WhatsApp button, and Order your card opens Bellver with your own referral link. Do not continue until this works.

4

Part 4

Add your domain on GitHub first

GitHub asks you to do this before you touch GoDaddy.

  1. Stay on Settings, Pages.
  2. Under Custom domain, type yourdomain.com (no www, no https) and click Save.

A "DNS check unsuccessful" warning is normal at this point. It clears after the next part.

5

Part 5

Point your GoDaddy domain to GitHub

DNS (Domain Name System) is your domain's address book. You only edit records: your name servers stay as they are.

When you finish, your DNS records must match this table:

TypeNameValue
A@185.199.108.153
A@185.199.109.153
A@185.199.110.153
A@185.199.111.153
CNAMEwwwYOUR-USERNAME.github.io
  1. Sign in to GoDaddy and open your Domain Portfolio.
  2. Click your domain, then open the DNS tab.
  3. Along the way GoDaddy may show offers or adverts. You do not need them: click Skip, Skip for now or No thanks to carry on to the DNS page.
  4. Find the A record named @ (it often says Parked). Click Edit, change the Value to 185.199.108.153 and click Save.
  5. Click Add New Record: Type A, Name @, Value 185.199.109.153, then Save. Repeat for 185.199.110.153 and 185.199.111.153.
  6. Find the CNAME record named www. Click Edit, set the Value to YOUR-USERNAME.github.io and click Save. No www record? Add one with those values.
  7. Check your list. Keep the four GitHub A records you just added and the www CNAME. If you see any other A record named @ with a different address (for example "WebsiteBuilder Site"), delete that extra one only.
  8. Some records, such as the NS (name server) records, cannot be edited or deleted. That is normal: leave them as they are.
  9. Switch off Forwarding if it is on.

GoDaddy may ask you to verify your identity when you save (Domain Protection). Complete it and continue. Leave your email records (MX and TXT) as they are.

6

Part 6

Wait, then switch on HTTPS

The padlock in the address bar.

  1. Wait. Changes usually show within an hour, but can take up to 24 hours (GoDaddy says up to 48 hours worldwide).
  2. Go back to GitHub, Settings, Pages, and refresh the page. The page does not update by itself, so it may still show the old warning until you refresh.
  3. When the DNS check turns green and says DNS check successful, your domain is connected.
  4. Still showing the warning? Wait a little and refresh again, or type your domain in Custom domain again and click Save.
  5. Tick Enforce HTTPS. Greyed out? GitHub is still preparing your certificate. Come back later.
  6. Open https://yourdomain.com and https://www.yourdomain.com. Both should show your Bellver website with the padlock.
  7. Tap Order your card once more and confirm your own referral link opens.

Done: your Bellver affiliate website now lives on your own domain.

Updating your website later

Your website is your own copy, so it does not change when MTG updates his pages. The videos are the exception: they play from bellveraffiliates.com and stay up to date by themselves.

  1. When MTG sends you a new zip, unzip it.
  2. In your repository, click Add file, then Upload files.
  3. Drag the contents in exactly as in Part 2. Files with the same name are replaced.
  4. Click Commit changes. Your website updates within about 10 minutes.

Never delete the file named CNAME in your repository. It holds your domain name.

If something goes wrong

"There isn't a GitHub Pages site here"

Wait 10 minutes. Check the repository name is exactly YOUR-USERNAME.github.io and Pages is set to main and / (root).

The page shows, but with no colours or images

The files went inside a folder. index.html must be at the top level (Part 2).

Your domain still shows a GoDaddy parked page

Check for an extra A record named @ that is not one of the four GitHub addresses, delete it, then wait up to 24 hours.

yourdomain.com works but www does not

The www CNAME value must be YOUR-USERNAME.github.io, with no https and no repository name.

GitHub still says DNS check unsuccessful

Refresh the GitHub Pages settings page. Still not green? Wait a little, or retype your domain in Custom domain and click Save. Enforce HTTPS stays greyed out until the check is green.

Your domain stopped working after an update

The CNAME file was deleted. Retype your domain under Settings, Pages, Custom domain, and click Save.
